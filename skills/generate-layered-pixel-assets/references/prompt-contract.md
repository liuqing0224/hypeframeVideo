# Prompt Contract

## Backdrop

- 16:9 pixel-collage environment base with paper or painted texture.
- No people, silhouettes, readable text, watermark, main landmark, or foreground frame.
- Leave central and lower space for composited layers.

## Environment Sheet

- Exactly three equal vertical columns in rear, architecture, foreground order.
- Perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` background, at least 6% pure-green gutters.
- Isolated complete layer in each column; no people, shadows, floor plane, text, or watermark.

## Character Sheet

- Exactly three equal vertical columns in primary, secondary, tertiary order.
- Full subject, action-specific pose, common ground line, complete props and limbs.
- Perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` background with wide gutters.
- No scenery, shadows, reflections, green clothing, text, or watermark.

Use `#ff00ff` only when a declared subject must be predominantly green, and record that key color before processing.
